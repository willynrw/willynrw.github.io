March 14 2026
Alessandra Hollowell will be performing Fae, for clarinet and electronics, at the Electronic Music Midwest Festival in Romeoville, Illinois US. Travel to this event is supported in part by the Manitoba Arts Council.

May 21-24 2026
My work Pastorale, for viola and electronics, will be performed at the Canadian University Music Society (MusCan) Annual Conference at the University of Manitoba. This conference also includes the Canadian Association of Music Libraries (CAML), the Canadian Society of Traditional Music (CSTM), and the Canadian Network for Musicians' Health and Wellness (CNMHW).

May 30 2026
My work Surfaces, for two violins, will be performed at Contemporary Music Interactive by Sophie Reimer-Epp and River Sawchyn. CMI is a new music series hosted in Winnipeg, Manitoba CA. I will also have the opportunity to lecture about my piece.

January 2027
I will premiere a new work at the abecede contemporary music festival in Bled Slovenia. I am participating in the precept.concept.percept composer's residency. Work in progress. Travel to this event is supported in part by the Manitoba Arts Council.
